## The eight that matter

1. **Katz threatens a new Gaza offensive; 1m to move south** — Israel's
   defence minister Israel Katz said on 26 Nov that Hamas must disarm under
   Trump's Gaza plan or face Operation "Gideon's Chariots II", including
   "another evacuation of one million Gaza residents southward". An overnight
   strike in Deir al-Balah killed Hamas political-bureau member Khaled Abu
   Nada and his wife. (Silent Dossier, 26 Nov; Inshorts, 26 Nov)
2. **Two tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz** — the IRGC said on 26 Nov
   that two tankers exploded and caught fire after hitting sea mines north of
   Oman's Khasab port; one was a supertanker carrying over 2m barrels of
   crude. UKMTO reported a projectile exploding near a tanker about 50
   nautical miles west of Dubai on 26 Nov, with no injuries or damage.
   (Reuters via Daily Fox News, 26 Nov; GlobalSecurity Iran War Day 272 update, 26 Nov)
3. **EU summit deadlocked on the €45bn Ukraine loan** — EU leaders meeting in
   Brussels on 26–27 Nov failed to agree the €45bn 2027 loan for Ukraine or
   the €1.582tn 2028–34 budget; Germany's Chancellor Merz threatened to veto
   the budget unless all 27 states back the loan. A decision is pushed to the
   December summit. (AP, 26 Nov; The Level Report, 26 Nov; Ground News, 26 Nov)
4. **Fresh strikes cut power in seven Ukrainian regions** — Ukrenergo said on
   the morning of 26 Nov that Russian strikes left consumers without power in
   Dnipropetrovsk, Zhytomyr, Zaporizhzhia, Mykolaiv, Odesa, Kharkiv and
   Chernihiv, with hourly outages. Kyiv was hit by ballistic missiles
   overnight, wounding seven. The Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant lost external
   power for the 17th time this year and runs on diesel generators.
   (Ukrenergo via MyUkraineIs, 26 Nov; Reuters via Devdiscourse, 26 Nov;
   Morning Briefing #228, 26 Nov)
5. **Trump's new tariffs hit 14 partners** — the US proposed 20% extra tariffs
   on China and 10–18% on the EU, Japan, India and 11 others. The USTR
   proposed tariffs of up to 25% on Indian goods, reducible to 17% if India
   signs a reciprocal trade agreement. (USA Today / Reuters, 25 Nov; Inshorts, 26 Nov)
6. **Cairo fuel-tanker fire kills at least 31** — a fuel tanker overturned on
   Cairo's ring road in Giza on 25 Nov and caught fire; at least 31 people
   died and about 21 were injured. (One Turn, 26 Nov; Inshorts, 26 Nov)
7. **Houthis strike Saudi ports; refinery claim denied** — Houthi forces
   attacked the Saudi Red Sea ports of Jeddah and Jazan on 25 Nov and claimed
   a strike on the Ras Tanura refinery; Saudi authorities said a projectile
   caused minor damage at Jazan with no casualties, a drone was destroyed
   over Jeddah, and four drones were destroyed over Ras Tanura.
   (GlobalSecurity Iran War Day 272 update, 26 Nov; Inshorts, 26 Nov)
8. **Thanksgiving: Rams win; tripleheader today** — the Los Angeles Rams beat
   the Green Bay Packers 26–13 in the Thanksgiving Eve game on 25 Nov. The
   Thanksgiving tripleheader on 26 Nov: Bears–Lions, Eagles–Cowboys,
   Chiefs–Bills. New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced the 100th
   Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade was rescheduled from Thursday to Saturday
   due to forecast heavy rain and strong winds. (247Sports / Bleacher Report,
   26 Nov; Inshorts, 26 Nov)
